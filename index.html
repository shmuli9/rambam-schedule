Rambam Yad — Learning Schedule

A personalised Mishneh Torah cycle on a difficulty-weighted word budget · pick your own start date and pace in the browser · Diaspora (UK) calendar

Each learning day costs roughly the same effort — words × the difficulty weight of the hilchot — so easy sefarim go faster (several perakim a day) and hard ones slower (part of a perek). Shabbos, Erev Shabbos, Yom Tov, Erev Yom Tov and Tisha B'Av are off; Chol HaMoed, Chanukah, Purim and Rosh Chodesh are learning days.

Open a schedule below and set the Start date and the Effort/day at the top — day numbers, dates, Hebrew dates, the completion date and the .ics export all re-compute instantly, in the browser, with nothing to install. Shabbosim and Yom Tov are skipped for you whatever day you start on.

How it works

  • Effort = word count × difficulty weight (data/weights.csv — 0.5 for read-throughs up to 1.5 for Shabbat, Ishut, Nizkei Mamon and friends). Edit it to taste.
  • Short/easy perakim are combined; long/hard ones are split at halacha boundaries; a single halacha never gets split.
  • The interactive tables carry the perek/word/weight data and enough Hebrew calendar to re-pace themselves offline — they are one self-contained file each, no dependencies. The embedded calendar covers start dates from 11 Oct 2026 to 12 Feb 2030.
  • In a schedule page use Export .ics for the pace you are looking at, or press Ctrl/Cmd+P for a landscape printout.
  • Everything is regenerated with python generate_schedule.py — see the README for flags (--budget, --start, --israel, --no-weights, …).
